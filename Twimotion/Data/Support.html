🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I create an animated GIF?

A: Simply type your text in the input field, select a theme, and tap "Export GIF". The app will create your animated GIF and save it to your Photos app.

Q: What's the difference between free and Pro?

A: Free users can create up to 2 GIFs per day. Pro users get unlimited GIFs, premium themes, and advanced customization options. You can try Pro with our 3-day free trial!

Q: How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

A: You can cancel your subscription anytime through your Apple ID account settings. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, find Twimotion, and tap "Cancel Subscription".

Q: Can I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

A: Yes! Tap "Restore Purchases" in the Pro upgrade screen, and your subscription will be restored automatically.

Q: Why is there a watermark on my GIFs?

A: The Twimotion watermark helps with brand recognition and is included on all GIFs. This is part of our app's design and cannot be removed.

Q: My GIF isn't saving to Photos. What should I do?

A: Make sure you've granted photo library access to Twimotion. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Twimotion and select "Add Photos Only" or "All Photos".

🐛 Troubleshooting

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Force close the app and restart it
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you're running the latest version of iOS
  • Update the app from the App Store

GIF Creation Issues

  • Check that your text isn't too long (500 character limit)
  • Ensure you have enough storage space on your device
  • Try restarting the app if GIF creation fails

Subscription Issues

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify your Apple ID is signed in
  • Try the "Restore Purchases" option
  • Contact Apple Support for billing issues
